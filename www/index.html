Settings

Farming Tick

Enter the minutes past the hour when your herb patches tick. Ticks are 20 minutes apart, so the other two fill in automatically.

My Patches

Tap to hide the patches you don't use. Hidden patches are left out of the timers, notifications and widgets.

Theme
Dark Mode Main theme
App icon Matches the theme
Battery saver Widgets update every 5 min, not every minute

Extras

h min

Deathpile
Nothing

Tap the card to start a 60 minute UIM deathpile, and again to cancel it. You get an alert when 15 minutes are left, and the bones turn red.

Run Stats

A run is counted whenever you plant patches. Plants less than 30 minutes apart count as the same run.
